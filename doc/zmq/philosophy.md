# The Zero in ZeroMQ

The [[zmq/philosophy]] of ZeroMQ starts with the zero. The zero is for 
- zero [[mq/broker]] (ZeroMQ is brokerless), 
- zero latency, 
- zero cost (it’s free), and 
- zero administration.

More generally, “zero” refers to the culture of minimalism that permeates the project. We add power by removing complexity rather than by exposing new functionality.
